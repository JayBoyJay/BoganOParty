# Map ideas

Each map is one file in `js/content/maps/`. Every idea below has its item (the "crown" you race to collect), the big landmarks to put around the board, and a twist that could make the board play differently. The twists are optional: they're map events or special spaces, so a map works without them.

| Map | Item | Landmarks | Board twist |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bathurst** (Mount Panorama) | Chequered flag from the 1000 | The Mountain circuit, Conrod Straight, The Chase, Skyline, Brock's Skyline lookout, camping hill with utes and eskies | The main loop *is* the circuit. "Conrod Straight" spaces give a speed boost (+3 to your next move). |
| **Kalgoorlie** | Golden nugget | The Super Pit (a giant terraced hole in the middle of the board), headframes, Hannan St pubs with skimpies, Questa Casa | A shortcut runs through the bottom of the Super Pit, but blasting times knock you back. |
| **Coober Pedy** | Opal | Dugout homes in the hillsides, mine shafts with "BEWARE: deep shafts" signs, the Big Winch, the underground church | Some spaces are open shafts: land on one and you drop to a lower route. |
| **Gold Coast** (Surfers Paradise) | Schoolies wristband | Q1 tower, the Surfers Paradise sign, theme-park coaster, beach with Meter Maids, Cavill Ave | A rip on the beach route drags everyone on it 2 spaces backwards each round. |
| **Darwin** | Croc tooth | Crocosaurus Cove's Cage of Death, Mindil Beach markets at sunset, giant termite mounds, a 2-litre Darwin Stubby | A croc patrols the river route and bites anyone it passes (−HP). |
| **Deniliquin Ute Muster** | Blue singlet | Rows of utes, a giant B&S bonfire, bull-riding arena, the Ute Muster stage | Ute spaces: hop in and get driven 5 spaces down a random road. |
| **The Big Things road trip** | Big Thing snow globe | Big Banana (Coffs), Big Prawn (Ballina), Big Pineapple, Big Merino (Goulburn), Big Lobster, Big Golden Guitar (Tamworth) | A long highway loop with one Big Thing per corner; each gives its own event. |
| **Melbourne** | Myki card | Flinders St Station, the MCG, trams on the streets, graffiti laneways, Brighton bathing boxes | Trams run along the main road and bump anyone in the way; four seasons in one day changes space colours each round. |
| **Sydney** | Bondi lifeguard whistle | Opera House, Harbour Bridge, Bondi Icebergs pool, Luna Park face | The Harbour Bridge is a toll road: crossing costs coins but saves 6 spaces. |
| **Hobart** | MONA ticket | MONA, kunanyi / Mt Wellington, Salamanca Market, Cascade Brewery | A ferry route across the Derwent runs every other round only. |
| **Uluru / Alice Springs** | Camel Cup trophy | Uluru, the Todd River (a dry riverbed), camels, the Henley-on-Todd boat race | The dry riverbed route is a bottomless-boat race: everyone on it moves together. |
| **Rottnest Island** (Perth) | Quokka selfie | Wadjemup lighthouse, quokkas everywhere, the ferry jetty, the Basin | Quokkas on spaces: stop next to one and you get a free selfie (a happy-face emote and coins). |
| **Bundaberg** | Bundy Bear | The Bundaberg distillery with its polar bear, cane fields, the Bargara turtles | Cane fires spread across the cane-field route each round. |

## Two to build next

- **Bathurst** suits a board game the best: the circuit already is a loop with natural forks (pit lane, The Chase), and a petrol-head map sits well next to the Toy Cars minigame.
- **Kalgoorlie** keeps the outback-mining feel of Broken Hill but turns it up, and the Super Pit gives the board a big centrepiece to look at.
